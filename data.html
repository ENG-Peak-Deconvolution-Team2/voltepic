Dataset

1,239 curves, four analytes, two coatings.

The ACS-ML-Biomarker data set (ML-Biomarker-Sensing): square-wave voltammograms of ascorbic acid, serotonin, estradiol and melatonin, alone and in mixtures, measured on polymer-coated chips. This page shows what was measured, how we split it, and the 125 held-out curves the analyzer ships with.

    Each raw file carries two working-electrode columns, i1 and i2. The source documentation calls i2 a reverse current; measured, it is a second working electrode (median Pearson r = 0.992 between the two).
    Cohort used here

    1,239 curves in 455 blocks.

    A block is the replicate measurements of one mixture, on one chip, on one day. Splits never cut through a block, so replicates of a curve are never on both sides of a train–test line.

    Two coatings

    Same analytes, very different curves.

    On PVDF the single-analyte peaks are separated, so a peak window can be drawn for each analyte. On P4VP all four crowd into roughly 0.13–0.22 V.
