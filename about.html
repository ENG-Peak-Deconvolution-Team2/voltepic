About

Part of ENG Peak Deconvolution.

An engineering design project on reading analyte information from voltammetric signals with overlapping peaks. Two teams work on the same data, splits and metrics — Team 1 with deep learning, Team 2 with classical machine learning. VoltEPIC — Explainable Prediction of Individual Concentrations — is Team 2's analysis site (work package 6).
