Limits

Where it should not be trusted.

  • One model, one partition. A single fitted model and a single split (seed 0). Spread across refits and partitions was not measured.
  • Held-out curves come in blocks. Replicates of one mixture sit in the same block, so the 125 curves are not independent samples.
  • Only seen these chips. ACS-ML-Biomarker data: P4VP / PVDF coatings, CHI760F, SWV 25 mV, 5 Hz. Its probabilities do not signal when a curve is from elsewhere; a feature z-score check could not separate such curves, because held-out curves already reach max |z| ≈ 950.
  • Boundary effects. A 6-level DWT on 200 points has them: 30 of the 228 coefficients depend almost only on the first or last 10 points and carry 9–20% of the model's split gain.
  • Saturating probabilities. P pins near 0 and 1, so explanations are read on the log-odds scale.
  • Detection only. The model says present or absent; it gives no concentration estimate.
