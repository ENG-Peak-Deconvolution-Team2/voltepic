    How a decision is made

    From a current trace to four answers, in under a millisecond.

    Every step below is computed live from one held-out curve, with the same code the analyzer uses.

    1. 01

      Measure

      Square-wave voltammetry, 0.004–0.800 V in 4 mV steps: 200 currents.

    2. 02

      Denoise

      A wavelet threshold removes the noise and keeps the peak shape.

    3. 03

      Decompose

      Seven wavelet bands, 228 coefficients, from coarse shape to fine detail.

    4. 04

      Vote

      Four boosted forests, 300 trees each, build up the evidence tree by tree.

    5. 05

      Decide

      Each probability is compared with a threshold chosen on training data.

    Explainability

    It looks where the peaks are.

    Shuffle one 20 mV window across all held-out curves and measure how much worse the model gets: that is permutation importance, the most reliable of the four explanation methods we tested. On PVDF, the single most important window of each analyte sits inside that analyte's peak window.
